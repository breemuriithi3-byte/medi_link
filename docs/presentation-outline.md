# Hackathon presentation outline

1. **The gap** — finding trusted care and keeping care information organized is difficult, especially when services are fragmented.
2. **The product** — MediLink AI is a privacy-first care access layer for patients, providers and administrators.
3. **Live journey** — patient account → safe health question → facility discovery → appointment request → private document → reminder.
4. **Provider loop** — role-scoped provider access makes appointment management actionable without exposing unrelated patient records.
5. **Responsible AI** — education, uncertainty, emergency escalation and clinician handoff are built into the experience.
6. **Cloud architecture** — Next.js on DigitalOcean App Platform, Managed PostgreSQL, private Spaces and short-lived signed URLs.
7. **Impact and next steps** — verified facility registry, local language support, SMS reminders, clinical review and Kenyan data protection readiness.
